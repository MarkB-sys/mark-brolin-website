How the work begins

Frame the question. Test the consensus. Decide what changes.

Advisory work starts with the decision, exposure or assumption that needs clearer treatment. The work then weighs structural forces, counterforces, institutional adaptation, timing and the implications for action.

The output is designed to be used: a more proportionate view of risk, a clearer set of priorities and a practical basis for choosing what to do next.

01 · Frame

Clarify the live decision, the assumptions behind it and what would materially change the choice.

02 · Test

Separate signal from noise and examine the counterforces, incentives and timelines consensus may miss.

03 · Decide

Translate the analysis into priorities for strategy, capital, risk and leadership.
